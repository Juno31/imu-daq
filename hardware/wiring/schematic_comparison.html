Unchanged

  • Teensy 4.1 as the IMU controller with 7 IMUs on two TCA9548A muxes: mux A on Wire (pins 18/19, 4 IMUs), mux B on Wire1 (pins 17/16, 3 IMUs), both at 400 kHz. The two buses are read in parallel; the timing simulation gave 200 frames/s (worst frame 4.7 of 5 ms), while one bus overran even at 100 frames/s.
  • Frame SYNC: 3.3 V toggle per frame on pin 2, recorded by the NI DAQ as an analog channel to align IMU frames with EMG.
  • NI DAQ records the analog signals and sends them to the PC over USB.
