SID Lab spine study (frozen design): 7 BNO086 pods C7–S1 on two TCA9548A muxes and a Teensy 4.1 logger, 4 paraspinal EMG + 1 ECG on MyoWare 2, an NI USB-6003, two 10 m Cat6 tethers, and a laptop on battery. The Teensy frame timer is the master clock; its SYNC edge is recorded on AI4.

Solid blue: I2C (Qwiic) · grey: USB · dashed / thick red: SYNC + Cable B · orange: EMG / ECG analog + Cable A. Pods 1–4 (C7–T9) on Mux A ports 0–3 and pods 5–7 (T12–S1) on Mux B ports 0–2, confirmed by the lab.

Event marker: the GUI Mark key pulses USB-6003 digital output P0.0; a short jumper on the DAQ terminal block feeds it to AI6, so each marker is sampled on the same 2 kHz clock as EMG and SYNC (±0.5 ms). This replaces the hub Qwiic Button (frozen in the brief; confirm with the senior before removing its cutout in brace_v4.scad).

Safety: the laptop runs on battery while recording (no mains loop). Never plug a powered Link Shield lead into a Cable Shield jack; never switch MyoWare modes while powered.
