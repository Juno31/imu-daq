Sensor placement

Mount each IMU flat on the spinous process. Board orientation does not matter: calibration finds it.

Calibration

  1. Neutral. Subject stands upright, arms relaxed, looking ahead. Hold still.
  2. Flexion axis. Slowly bend forward about 40–60° and come back up.
Do step 1, then step 2.

Recalibrate after an IMU restart or a board reset: the sensors' heading reference restarts with them.
